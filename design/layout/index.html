Before your first swim

Ask us anything at the slipway, or send a request below and we will answer by email.

How cold is it?

Between 8 and 18 degrees depending on the month. We shorten swims in winter.

Do I need a wetsuit?

No. Many members swim without one; the course covers both.

What if the sea is rough?

We cancel by 6 am and post it on this page.
